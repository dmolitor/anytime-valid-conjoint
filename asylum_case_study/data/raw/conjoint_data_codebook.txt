This codebook pertains to the conjoint_data files, which contain the study conjoint data. Rows correspond to conjoint profiles evaluated by a respondent. See study Supplementary Information (SI) appendix for more details on variable measurement.


ResponseId: Anonymized respondent identifier.

task: Conjoint task (pair) number, where A denotes the first pair, B denotes the second pair, and so on.

prof: Conjoint profile number, where A1 denotes the first profile (1) in the first pair (A), A2 the second profile in the first pair, B1 the first profile (1) in the second pair (B), and so on.

pref: Indicator for whether or not a profile was chosen in the pair, based on the forced choice.

rate: Individual rating of the profile, where 1 denotes "absolutely send the applicant back" and 7 denotes "definitely allow the applicant to stay".

ratebin: Dichotomized version of rate indicating acceptance of a profile, where values of rate greater than 4 are assigned 1 for ratebin, and 0 otherwise.

cconsist: Asylum Testimony conjoint profile attribute. See the study Materials and Methods section and the Extended Data section (Table ED1) for more information on the conjoint attributes.

cgender: Gender attribute.

corigin: Country of Origin attribute.

cage: Age attribute.

cjob: Previous Occupation attribute.

cvulner: Vulnerability attribute.

creason: Reason for Migrating attribute.

creligion: Religion attribute.

clang: Language Skills attribute.
